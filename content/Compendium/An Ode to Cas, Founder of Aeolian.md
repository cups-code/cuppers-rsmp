Lost in the universe
Not knowing what I would do
I used to believe that I'd be alone
WIthout anyone to look onto
But when she took me in
Into Aeolian
Oh, it felt like fate
That I wouldn't be alone in this world

She was my mother
A warmth, so fulfilling
But when death took her so quickly
I begged, maybe, just maybe

Thank you, mother
For guiding me out of despair
And now, I think I can stand on my own
'Cause of you, I'll be fine on my own

Thank you, mother
Be safe in the stars
If only I could return
To try to hear your voice again
Just to find you again

Thank you, goodbye
Farewell, farewell
Thank you, goodbye
I'll see you later, when I return
