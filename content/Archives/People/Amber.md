> Amber originated from the Desert Kingdom, being an only child with both of her parents being hunters. Out of inspiration from her family, she acquired the job as a hunter when she is around her teens (17yrs) At this time, the whole family is full human and nothing seems to be out of the ordinary until Amber had a dream to become a dragon; an “impossible” dream to be exact. 
> 
> It was one day that she met Lilith and fulfilled the dream for Amber by fusing draconic dna with hers via machine. In turn, Amber would be helping Lilith with other experiments. However she didn’t disclose who was getting experimented and it was Amber all along. Due to this, she didn’t consent and tried to escape using her wings. As a result, she failed and her wings gotten cut off. Days to months have passed and Amber did eventually escape on her own secretly. 
> 
> Due to the draconic side of her, dragon-like behaviors started to fester within her and she was accused of stealing gold from the Desert kingdom’s treasury while she was just staring at it. She was then exiled and she moved to the Nether kingdom where her parents reside due to gaining a job opportunity from the hunting guild. Amber abandoned her home back at the desert kingdom and moved in with her family, only to see that her mom unfortunately passed away due to illness while she was gone. Her father, too, was missing days after she moved in.
