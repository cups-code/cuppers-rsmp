> [[Cas]] Nova found a quiet valley far from all kingdoms and other civilizations. The Ekoran Mountains, the name born of her lost realm, and Aeolian were made. Aeolian, the word that means relating to or arising from the action of the wind. Pronounced ae-o-li-an. Cas missed Ekora, missed her people, her family, her lover, all gone. All that remained of her home was the flowers on her skin and her painful memories. Aeolian is a place of comfort and peace for all nature creatures and humans to come together as one. Aeolian is not a place for war or conflict, but will always protect nature and the animals a part of it. However, a darkness under Aeolian grows full of secrets and sculk who may uncover it and who may join it?

# Solrayian Church
> Cas has always been a spiritual fairy. In Ekora, her main deity of worship was the war and warrior god, Wari. Now in Aeolian, the light of a strong god/goddess of the sun has blessed the land. This blessing from the church allows crops to grow stronger for a more bountiful harvest. 

> All religions and people are welcome in Aeolian. For your path will always be your own. No matter the religion, it is respected; no fighting will be allowed between religions, or Cas will put a stop to it.
