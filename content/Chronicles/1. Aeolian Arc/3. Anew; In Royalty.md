After an arduous journey from his hometown, Aeolian, he had finally felt the familiar sands of the Desert Kingdom upon him. Walking up to the newly-built government building, it was to his surprise that someone was already expecting his arrival. "Eiko", the Hand of the Viceroy.

Except, it wasn't Eiko. Upon discussion, it was quickly revealed that Cuppers was talking to Rev instead. They both continued to discuss the arrangement of his bardic position and catch up on what's happened in the Desert Kingdom.

At the end of the day, his position was now fully established. "Royal Bard" of the Desert Kingdom. Months of waiting had now finally paid off. And this, was only the beginning of a new story to come.
