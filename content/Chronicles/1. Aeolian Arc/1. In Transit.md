Months had passed since his performance at the Dragon's Nest tavern, the time when things started turning around for him and his career. He hid away in old shops, other taverns, while he wrote songs and performed to small crowds. He still hadn't received his contract from the Desert Kingdom, and believed that maybe it wasn't the right time yet.

It was late in the night, as he strolled around the footpath to his next adventure, 'till he found a poster of his late mother's passing. He couldn't believe the sight of it. His caretaker, who looked so strong and who he idolised in his head, had been taken so suddenly by death themself. 

He rushed back to Aeolian, thus finding that the news were not merely rumours, but the truth. He couldn't believe it. He wasn't there, he was so busy, he fell in love with his music that he abandoned the one person who take care of him since the start. The one who founded the town he resided in during his childhood and early adolescence. Now, all that was left, was a gravestone, and a various assortment of flowers.

Holding his glacial and starry tears under his animated face, he wrote a song in the spur of the moment.
