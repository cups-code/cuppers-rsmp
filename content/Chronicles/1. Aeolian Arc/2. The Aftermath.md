Since the passing of his late mother, he realised that he'd have to pick his life back up. He knew that he had some unfinished business to do before returning back to the Desert Kingdom, in order to inquire about his pending offer.

After days of isolating himself with his guitar, he decided to take one final look at his mother's memorial before packing his things in order to start his journey back to the Desert Kingdom. When he approached her memorial, he was frightened by what *goopy*, disgusting mess had spread from where her body had been buried. Then, he realised. Sculk. It had been spreading to parts of Aeolian, and her memorial was only the start.

The only thing he knew about this wretched infection was to not touch it or go near it, but he wasn't exactly sure on what to do next. He frantically searched for Amber, who had been away on a meeting in the Nether Kingdom as the new Hand of Viceroy to address this very problem. When she returned to Aeolian, Cuppers immediately ran to inform her about the recent situation. Both confused and shocked, he took a step back while Amber continued to assess the situation, who was also trying to process it at the same time.

Although he would've wanted to stay for a little longer to take care of this issue with Amber, he had to return to the Desert Kingdom to ensure that they knew he was still alive and to know about what's happening to his pending position as Royal Bard. He packed his things, set out a course for where to go (making sure he had a few rest stops and a bit of time to check out other towns on the way), and headed out.

It was going to be a long journey back, but he knew that this was the right path for him. Cas would've wanted this. He would do it for her, for all of Aeolian, to pay back to the town that raised him. This was only the beginning.
