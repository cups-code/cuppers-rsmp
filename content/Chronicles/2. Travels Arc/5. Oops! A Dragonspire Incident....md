After hours of travel, he finally made it to Dragonspire. It was a town he was curious about, knowing absolutely nothing other than a map of the location. He made his way to the hotel, interacting with a receptionist that seemed to always describe the hotel as “*the best hotel ever*”. He was quite concerned after the receptionist had not only referenced things that he never knew (“*what’s a K-pop idol?*”) and the fact that they were infected. 

Both Cups and the receptionist continued to discuss various things, especially in regards to the hotel. However, during their conversation, Cups noticed that the both of them were being quietly observed. After discretely trying to discern who the individual was, he noticed that it was Vivian again. He knew that he was in trouble, and that he had to leave as soon as possible.

He echoed the words, “*I should’ve just gone to Whitepetal, I should’ve just gone to Whitepetal…*”, as he regretted his decision of coming to Dragonspire. He didn’t want to get into trouble and he didn’t want to be around the infected either. With haste, he fled from the town, making his way back to the Desert Kingdom.

---

After returning to the Desert Kingdom, he quickly made his way to the Desert Government building. He went to the garden, trying to find Rev, and luckily enough, they were there. He swiftly informed Rev about the occurrences within Dragonspire, with some other chatter regarding talking to the Great Magister to aid him in self-defense, and matters regarding the Gladiator Event happening in the near future.
