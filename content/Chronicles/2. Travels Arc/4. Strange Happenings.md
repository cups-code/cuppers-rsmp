It wasn’t a typically abnormal day in the sunny desert. Cups was only settling in, whether that was by appreciating the garden on the roof of the Desert Government building or making preparations to travel to other towns for networking purposes. Nevertheless, it came to his surprise that there were new faces in the Desert.

However, he noticed that… it was quite odd that there *were* new faces in the Desert. From what he had heard, the Desert Kingdom required visas in order to enter the Desert, but these two strange individuals had just entered for the sake of it.

They were quickly stopped by a member of the Desert Government, someone that Cups hadn’t particularly talked to. It was revealed that one of those individuals was named Vivian, and he’d be sure to take note of it. Watching the encounter from the sidelines, he was frightened by Vivian’s charisma and control over their hostage, and he made sure to be cautious while observing.

Even with this arrival of atypical faces, he continued on with his day after they left. Packing his bags and instruments required, he made his way to the town of Dragonspire.
