# The Spatial Chronicles

> [!Warning]
> The content and information within the The Spatial Chronicles should be canon, but the documents themselves are not considered to be part of the lore. This is used as a tool to recollect information on previous events within R!Cuppers’ lore journey.

Preserved by the space juice holding Cuppers’ memories and life-force, it has documented every memory and important pieces of information into separate, readable chronicles.
